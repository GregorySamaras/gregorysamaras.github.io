Area of work

Information Protection

My work covers how information is classified, protected, retained and recovered across Microsoft 365. I research technical options, prepare and present recommendations to Security leadership, IT management, Legal and Compliance, and implement the agreed controls.

Microsoft Purview — Classification & Data Protection

I investigated Microsoft Purview's information protection capabilities and presented the findings to Security. The evaluation informed our classification and protection design, which I helped shape and implemented.

I deployed the sensitivity labelling solution across Microsoft 365, including encryption, authenticated external sharing and restrictions on copying, printing and exporting protected information.

I also implemented Data Loss Prevention (DLP) policies and reviewed outbound email activity to understand where company information was being shared externally. In one investigation, the controls exposed transfers to personal accounts; I provided the evidence to the responsible team for action.

Email Retention & Archiving

During the earlier adoption of Microsoft 365, Litigation Hold had been used to preserve email for up to ten years, without a clear approach to managing different legal retention requirements.

Over time, deleted and modified items accumulated in Recoverable Items, putting pressure on Exchange Online storage. I investigated this behaviour and evaluated Microsoft Purview retention policies, retention labels and Mimecast email journaling and archiving as alternatives to broad, long-term mailbox holds.

I presented the technical options and trade-offs to help inform decisions about how the organisation should retain email for legal and business purposes.

Microsoft 365 Backup — Recovery of Critical Data

Retaining information and recovering it quickly are different requirements. I assessed Microsoft 365 Backup for business-critical accounts and data, prioritised recovery needs, performed restoration tests and measured recovery times against existing options.

The results supported my recommendation to use Microsoft 365 Backup for selected critical data alongside existing archiving, improving the ability to recover quickly from deletion or administrative mistakes.

SharePoint — Document Protection & Records Management

A more recent initiative involves protecting and managing business documents stored in SharePoint, with requirements covering classification, DLP, retention policies and labels, records management, auditing and eDiscovery.

My responsibility covers assessing these requirements against the existing Microsoft Purview configuration and implementing the appropriate controls alongside Security and external delivery partners.

This includes reconciling new requirements with the classification model, identifying technical and licensing dependencies, and determining which changes require privileged access to the Microsoft 365 environment.
