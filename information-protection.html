Area of work

Information Protection

I have led technical evaluations and implemented Microsoft 365 information protection controls, while examining how retention, archiving and recovery should meet both business and legal requirements.

Classification and data loss prevention

I investigated Microsoft Purview’s information protection capabilities and presented findings that shaped the organisation’s classification approach. I implemented the resulting model, including sensitivity labels, encryption, authenticated external sharing and restrictions on copying, printing and exporting protected information.

I also implemented Data Loss Prevention (DLP) policies and reviewed outbound email activity. The monitoring also produced actionable findings around external data flows, which I investigated and documented for further action by the relevant teams.

Email retention and archiving

During the earlier adoption of Microsoft 365, ten-year Litigation Holds had become a broad way of preserving email. Over time, deleted and modified items accumulated in Recoverable Items, creating storage and operational pressure in Exchange Online.

I investigated the underlying retention behaviour and evaluated Purview retention policies and labels. I also led the evaluation and coordinated implementation of Mimecast journaling and archiving to address long-term retention requirements.

Microsoft 365 Backup and recovery

I assessed Microsoft 365 Backup alongside alternatives including Veeam and Rubrik, considering cost, coverage, complexity and recovery needs. I prioritised critical data, ran restoration tests and compared recovery performance.

The results supported the adoption of Microsoft 365 Backup for selected high-priority data alongside existing archiving. The decision addressed a different requirement: restoring access quickly after deletion or an administrative mistake.

SharePoint document governance

My more recent work covers applying Purview to business documents in SharePoint, including retention labels, classification, DLP, auditability and eDiscovery. I translate those requirements into technical configurations while aligning them with the protection model already in place.
